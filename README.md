# Jumper_Summer

Description:
Jumper summer is a platformer about manipulating your environment and making use of "nature" (i..e. wind) to traverse your way to the mountain.

Credits/Tools:
I used Aseprite for the art. REAPER for mixing audio from Pixabay, and Godot game engine.
Most of the art is mine, some such as the clouds and brick tilemap are from itch.io.

Context: 
The game where I mixed everything I have learned from Terra and my past projects so far and also the new knowledge I have from Hack Club Haven. The game is a platformer where the player needs to utilize the environment to finish it.
