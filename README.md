<h3> Story: </h3>
Jumper summer is a platformer about manipulating your environment and making use of "nature" (i..e. wind) to traverse your way to the mountain.

<h3> Context: </h3>
The game where I mixed everything I have learned from Terra and my past projects so far and also the new knowledge I have from Hack Club Haven. The game is a platformer where the player needs to utilize the environment to finish it.

<h3> Controls: </h3>
ESC - Pause
A - Move left
S - Move down
D - Move right
Spacebar -  Jump
*Use the barrels and the fans to navigate through the game. They are meant to me moved or used.

<h3> Play: </h3>
It can be played at: 
https://matt-not-found.itch.io/jumper-summer

<h3> Tools: </h3>
I used Godot and Gdscript, for the art I used Aseprite/Libresprite, and I also used REAPER and Pixabay for the audio.
