Carta Bianca is a bold poster script with an inline detail and a confident forward lean.

The strokes lean hard on the downstroke and run thin on the way back up, with a clean inline running through the heavier parts that catches light across the wider letters. Caps loop into the next letter without much pause; the lowercase keeps a steady rhythm beneath the show. The whole design is built for size — it carries a vintage hand-painted weight, the kind of brushwork that used to land on record sleeves, theatre marquees, and grocery-store windows.

Carta Bianca ships as a layered family — Regular, Plain, and Shadow — three matching faces designed to stack. Set the same word in all three using identical size and position; the layers combine into a single typographic logo with depth, drop, and inline highlight. Pair colors freely: a bright Plain over a dark Shadow, or a metallic foil over a deep ground.

Designed and created by Mans Greback.

The font is built with advanced OpenType functionality and guaranteed top-notch quality, containing stylistic and contextual alternates, ligatures and more automatic and manual features; all to give you full control and customizability.
It has extensive lingual support, covering all Latin-based languages, from North Europa to South Africa, from America to South-East Asia. It contains all characters and symbols you'll ever need, including all punctuation and numbers.
